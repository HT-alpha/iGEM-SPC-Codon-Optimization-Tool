## What was timed

**Tier 3 repair.** `repair` still runs one `ConstraintChecker::check` per
synonym trial. On yeeJ assembly, standard, 4 generations, the profile is 5
calls and 0.558 s. The published row for that job was 5 calls and 0.600 s.
Seed repair (`climb`, `kmer_replace`, `ascend`, `gc_delta`) was not edited.

**Structure `l0_batch`.** The yeeJ structure job still folds twice. This run
records `l0_batch` 2 calls, 0.668 s, and the same fold counts as the pin.
The release note in [`tier3-kmer-cache.md`](tier3-kmer-cache.md) recorded
0.656 s for 2 calls on a `--release` build of an older vector. This dev-profile
run does not beat that release number, and the fold was not skipped. The
structure job wall is 4.143 s against the published 3.956 s.

**CAI-only ViennaRNA.** A job that does not score
`structure.local_accessibility` does not call `structure_diag.annotate`.
yeeJ empty CAI drops from 0.533 s to 0.081 s. The v2.9.2 profile attributed
about 0.446 s of a CAI-only yeeJ assembly job to one `pfl_fold_up`.

**GATE batch.** The 50 CAI-only designs run on a thread pool, one artifact
store per gene. Wall goes from 5.899 s to 0.758 s. The designs do not fold,
so the threads do not share ViennaRNA's process-global temperature.
