## What changed

### Zero-weight CAI search (`DEP-SRCH-020`)

The arithmetic CAI delta of a single codon is 0 whenever the incumbent still
has another counted weight of 0. A sum of those deltas cannot see that
removing one `AGG`, or avoiding a new one, raises the true CAI once the last
zero is gone. Beam ties are then arbitrary, and the search can introduce
`AGG` at arginine sites that the native CDS did not use.

The reported score is still the geometric mean. Tiers 1 and 2 now build
their additive table from `surrogate_delta_table`. For `usage.cai`, that
table is the arithmetic delta when the incumbent has no zero weight, and
the change in the sum of log-weights otherwise. A zero weight is entered as
−1e6, below `ln(w)` for every `w` in `(0, 1]`. Tier 2 still accepts or
rejects the block on the reported score.

On a CAI-only job, a block that removes some but not all zeros does not
change the reported CAI, so it is accepted, and a later block can remove
the rest. A second objective can still reject that partial step when the
reported scalar gets worse while CAI stays 0. The phase 2 multi-objective
inputs have no zero-weight codon, so they stay on the arithmetic path.

| CDS | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| aceB, 534 codons, native `AGG` at 14 | CAI 0, one designed `AGG`, 0.421 s | CAI 1.0, no in-frame `AGG`, 0.137 s |
| thrA, 821 codons, native `AGG` at 28 and 243 | CAI 0, eight designed `AGG`, 0.694 s | CAI 1.0, no in-frame `AGG`, 0.197 s |

Both after-runs use the gate budget (`fast`, 4 generations, seed 7) and
translate to the input protein. `run_cai_only` on all 50 proteins reports

`benchmarks/corpus/cai_only.json` changed, from 0.0 to 1.0. The other 48
`codonforge_cai` values are the previous file.

`GATE-BEN-001` is this CAI-only claim. It is not the MFE comparison against
LinearDesign or CDSfold.

### Structure evaluations

`FoldSession` keeps the accessibility penalty for sequences a job has
already folded. An identical CDS is returned as stored. A sequence within
`max(1, length/160)` codon edits of a stored parent is rebuilt with
`AccessibilityCache.substitute`, which matches a full fold on the windows
outside the edit. Anything further away is folded from scratch. The session
is not checkpointed; a resumed job folds again.

On the two structure jobs above, the session counters were:

| job | hits | full folds | local codon edits | wall |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| rpsL, CAI + structure | 23 | 5 | 25 | 0.561 s |
| waaS, CAI + structure | 27 | 3 | 37 | 2.255 s |

Before this session, the waaS run made 48 `pfl_fold_up` calls (2.375 s of a
4.252 s profile) for 21 unique sequences. The knee vector is the same.

## Left refused

- `epistasis.dwell_deviation` stays `CF-LAND-001`. No dwell offset was fitted.
- `GET /v1/recalibrations` and the host-catalogue routes stay `CF-CLI-003`.
- A Potts request for a non-E. coli host, or a missing MG1655 artifact, stays
  `CF-LAND-001`. No distance penalty is applied.
- No gene-copy number or s-value was added. `design_operon` still uses the
  caller's fluxes. Joint search is not NSGA-III.
- The original MFE/CAI frontier is not claimed.
