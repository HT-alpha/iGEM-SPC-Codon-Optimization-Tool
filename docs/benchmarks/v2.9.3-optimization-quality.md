# How good the search is, measured on the v2.9.2 tree

Same tree, host, weights and checker as
`docs/benchmarks/v2.9.3-external-comparison.md`. This note does not
compare tools. It asks whether DNA Design Suite's own answer is optimal,
stable, or an accident of the seed, and where a weak answer comes from.

Command:

```sh
.venv/bin/python tools/optimization_quality.py \
    --out docs/benchmarks/v2.9.3-quality-rows.json
```

Jobs use the code defaults, recorded from the runs: tier-1 beam 32,
4 tier-1 seeds, tier-2 block 8, 4 sweeps, population 12. Budget `fast`
is tier 1 and tier 2. Budget `standard` adds tier 3. A generation count
of 0 does not run an NSGA generation. The stagnation window is 21 unless
a row says 8, so a cap of 4 or 12 is what stops the run, except the one
row that asks for 12 generations with the default window of 8.

## The exact baseline, and the check that it is exact

Unconstrained CAI separates by codon. The optimum is the per-codon
maximum of relative adaptiveness, ties broken toward the lexicographically
smaller codon. That is `cai_independent.most_frequent`, stops left as
written. With family maxima of 1, that CAI is 1 on every gene in this set.

Restriction sites, homopolymers and fixed motifs are regular. A DP whose
state is the last 7 nucleotides can carry NotI (8) and every shorter
pattern. GC windows and scattered 12-mers are not in that state. The DP
CAI is then an upper bound. If the DP sequence also passes the full
checker, the bound is the optimum of the full set, because nothing
feasible for the tighter set can score above the relaxed one.

The DP was checked against exhaustive search on
`ATGCATAATTTTTATAAAGATGAATAA` (M H N F Y K D E *), 384 codon assignments.
Planted pattern `ATGCAC` plus homopolymers of 7.
Brute CAI 0.8665721033097589, DP CAI 0.8665721033097589,
`dp_matches_brute` is true,
the unconstrained DP matches the closed form at 1.0,
and the planted pattern binds (the constrained CAI is strictly lower).
On the local-DP rows the largest absolute difference between the DP log-sum
and `N log(CAI)` is 1.354472090042691e-14. A DP that disagrees with the
brute force aborts the script before any gene number is written.

full checker on every gene below, so the CAI is an upper bound only.
DNA Design Suite's standard, 4-generation, seed-7 CAI is the achieved column.
The difference is how far the design sits under a sequence that is not

rpsL, elastin, waaS and yeeJ are flat from `fast` generations 0 through
standard 12. yagF is the exception, and it is small: generations 0,
either budget, return 0.9286425884184984, which is the v2.9.1 `fast`

0.928716785416348, and 12 generations stay there. The other four seeds
at 4 generations stay on the generations-0 value. So "more generations"
helped once, on one seed, by 7.4e-5, and then stopped. It did not help
yeeJ at all. That is not premature convergence in the sense of a cap that
cut the run off while CAI was still climbing. It is a basin that tier 3,
as configured, does not leave.

The mechanism, from the warnings on these jobs:

- `CF-SRCH-017`: tier 1 could not carry a non-local constraint, and the
  sequence later tiers started from was built by seed repair. GC windows
  and scattered 12-mers are not regular. The beam never sees them. A
  wider beam does not fix this refusal.
- Seed repair is deterministic. Ties break by higher relative
  adaptiveness, then by the smaller codon index (`DEP-SRCH-015`).
- `CF-SRCH-001` on `potts_guided` and on `block_gibbs`: no epistatic
  context model is registered, so those two operators are recorded as
  unavailable rather than approximated (`DEP-SRCH-005`, ticket P3-05).
  The planned mix is uniform synonymous 0.35, Potts 0.35, block Gibbs
  0.20, violation-directed 0.10. The mix that ran is the first and the
  last, renormalized. Once the seed is feasible, violation-directed has
  nothing to repair, and uniform synonymous is what remains to hunt for
  a higher CAI inside the GC windows and the unique-12-mer rule.
- Population 12. The archive records mutations. They do not replace the
  reported CAI unless they improve it. On these single-objective runs
  they do not, except the one yagF step.

## Pareto fronts

Two real objectives, assembly, seed 7, standard, stagnation window 21.
`usage.gc_content` is not an axis: it is diagnostic and has no biological
direction. Burden is `burden.growth_cost`, minimise, a dimensionless
mismatch, not a growth rate. Structure is `structure.local_accessibility`,
minimise, the same windowed penalty as the external note, not global MFE.

Hypervolume here is recomputed with `indicator_hypervolume` on the
minimised vector. CAI is negated. The reference was fixed before looking
at a front: `(-CAI, burden)` against `[0.05, 5.0]`, and
`(-CAI, accessibility)` against `[0.05, 8.0]`. A point that does not
strictly dominate the reference contributes 0. None of the complete
points fell outside. The checkpoint's `hypervolume_history` is a different
series with the engine's own reference. It is quoted as history, not
compared with the column below.

A pair of front members was tested for domination in the reported senses.
`dominated_pairs` is 0 on every row. The reported fronts are
non-dominated. They are not all well spread.

0.09 to 0.10, hypervolume 4.637233334028193 to 4.652026457082369.
waaS grows from 2 points to 5, with a smaller rise. Elastin does not
have a useful burden tradeoff: at 12 generations the burden range is
4.866322900198838e-10 while CAI ranges by 0.011478772491524625, all of
it downward from the CAI-only value. The hypervolume stays at
1.9537184363. Extra points there are the same burden with a worse CAI.
The repeat protein's growth-cost term barely responds to the codon edits
that stay assembly-feasible.

## Verdict

The search is exact where the objective and the constraints are the
class tier 1 was built for, and it sticks to a repaired seed where they
are not.

- Unconstrained CAI: gap 0 on all 10 genes, at `fast` and generations 0,
  stable across seeds. Already optimal. The generations plateau on an
  empty constraint set is that fact.
- Golden Gate and BioBrick: gap 0 against the pattern DP on every gene
  of at most 2500 nt. Tie with the optimum, and with DNA Chisel where
  DNA Chisel also hits it.
- Assembly, including GC windows and 12-mers: feasible on this set,
  better than DNA Chisel, and not certified optimal. The only fully
  enumerated assembly instance (54 nt, 1188 feasible sequences) is also
  gap 0, found by repair and tier 2 before NSGA. On the natural genes
  the certificate stops at an upper bound. rpsL is within 0.00381 of a
  sequence that fails a single 12-mer. Elastin's 0.56 against CAI 1 is
  not a certificate.
- Seed 7 is stable on rpsL and elastin and is not the best seed on waaS
  or mCherry. Report a seed sweep when the constraint set contains a
  12-mer rule. One seed is a sample.
- More generations do not raise single-objective assembly CAI, except
  one 7.4e-5 step on yagF at seed 7 between generations 0 and 4. yeeJ
  is unchanged at 0, 4 and 12. The v2.9.1 note was right, and the cause
  is the basin, not a generation counter that never started. Tier 3's
  mutation mix, on this build, is uniform synonymous and
  violation-directed. Potts and block Gibbs are named as unavailable.
- Two objectives are where extra generations earn their cost. Fronts
  stay non-dominated, gain points, and the hypervolume rises. Elastin's
  CAI-plus-burden front is clumped on burden and should not be shown as
  a tradeoff. A structure request at generations 0 does not produce a
  structure coordinate.
- Seed repair does not prefer the native CDS over `cai_greedy` on these
  six genes. It prefers one deterministic CDS, and both starts become it.

What to fix, and what this study did not try:

The assembly basin is seed repair after `CF-SRCH-006`, followed by an
NSGA mix that is missing the two context operators. A larger tier-1 beam
does not address that, because tier 1 refuses before the beam is the
limit. Keeping more than one repaired seed, or registering the epistatic
model those operators require, is the mechanism to change. Neither was
run here, so neither is claimed to close the rpsL interval or to lift
elastin. No product bug turned up in the scorers, the checker, or the DP
cross-check. This change is the measurement.
